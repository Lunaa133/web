one day there was a cashier, her eyeshadow was glittery purple. she seemed a bit tense and i was afraid at first, but i told her her eyeshadow was beautiful anyway.

she didn't say anything and her face stayed sharp. but i kept smiling while looking at her, and then she smiled back — like, she was smiling for real. it was a genuine smile. we kept smiling at each other until i left the place :D

why project our insecurities on other people when we can simply use the same ways of communication to make someone's day? a simple compliment may sometimes be just the thing one needs to hear.
